# Example Dream: The Endless Staircase

I was in a building with no clear entrance. The hallways kept stretching longer the more I walked, and I found a spiral staircase made of pale stone that seemed to loop endlessly upward. 

There was no sun, only a sickly green light seeping from the cracks between the steps and walls. The air was damp and metallic, like old pipes. 

I had a strong feeling of longing and dread at the same time - I desperately wanted to reach the top, but I also knew something was waiting for me there. 

The stairs defied gravity slightly - sometimes I'd climb a few steps and find myself at a completely different landing than I should be.
