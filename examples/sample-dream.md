# Sample Dream

I was walking through a long corridor that kept getting narrower. The walls were made of wet concrete and kept breathing. There were no windows, only soft blue light coming from somewhere above. I could hear my own footsteps but they sounded far away. At the end of the corridor there was a large circular room with a ceiling so high I couldn’t see it. In the center there was a black staircase that went both up and down at the same time. I felt both calm and terrified.
